R07 — Phase 3: novel architectures (conditional on Phase 2 outcome)
====================================================================

Triggers
--------
If after Phase 2 the test 60-min MAE is still ≥ 3.20, switch to genuinely
new architectures. If ≥ 3.18, push these in parallel with Phase 2 results.

R07a — Mixup augmentation
-------------------------
For each batch, sample λ ~ Beta(0.5, 0.5) and a permuted batch order:
  x_mix = λ * x[i] + (1-λ) * x[perm[i]]
  y_mix = λ * y[i] + (1-λ) * y[perm[i]]
  mask_mix = mask[i] & mask[perm[i]]   (both must be valid)
Train STAEformer with mixup (probability 0.3 per batch). 4-seed ensemble.
Expected: −0.005 to −0.02 on test (mild regularizer).

R07b — STAEformer LoRA per-sensor
---------------------------------
Finetune existing 4-seed STAEformer checkpoints with rank-4 LoRA adapters
on the temporal-attention Q/K/V projections, one adapter per sensor (so
adaptive_emb gets a learnable "low-rank update" per sensor). Adds
~125K params total. Train 20 epochs at lr 5e-4, then ensemble.
Expected: −0.01 to −0.05.

R07c — Test-time training (TTT)
-------------------------------
Extend ST-TTC: instead of training only a calibrator, do a few gradient
steps on the LAST FEW LAYERS of STAEformer at test time. Specifically:
  - keep the encoder frozen
  - update the output_proj layer
  - one Adam(lr 1e-4) step per dequeued sample on masked-MAE objective
Risk: distribution drift. Mitigation: reset every N samples (N≈100).
Expected: −0.01 to −0.05 if it works.

R07d — Multi-resolution STAEformer
----------------------------------
Train two extra STAEformer instances on 15-min and 30-min aggregated inputs
(predict the same 5-min-output horizon). Three resolutions × ensemble.
Cost: 2 extra trainings × 30 min. Expected: −0.02 to −0.05.

R07e — Cross-prediction distillation
------------------------------------
After the super-ensemble is built, use it to generate "pseudo-targets" on
the val data, then add a self-distillation auxiliary loss to a fresh
STAEformer training. Ensemble teacher → distilled student. Expected:
−0.01 to −0.04 IF the student matches the teacher on val.

Sequencing
----------
Default order (if Phase 2 leaves us > 3.20):
  R07a (mixup) — cheapest, smallest blast radius
  R07b (LoRA) — finetune existing, ~1h
  R07d (multi-res) — separate trainings, ~1.5h
  R07c (TTT) — eval-only, ~5 min
  R07e (distillation) — last, ~1h
