R02 — Bigger STAEformer (d_model=192, L=4, FFN=384) + stronger reg
====================================================================

Hypothesis
----------
Seed std=0.004 on test 60-min says architecture is at a structural plateau
*for d_model=152*. The plateau may break with more capacity if combined
with stronger regularization to keep overfitting in check.

Setup
-----
Train 1 seed at scaled config:
- input_embedding_dim = 32 (was 24)
- tod_embedding_dim   = 32 (was 24)
- dow_embedding_dim   = 32 (was 24)
- adaptive_embedding_dim = 96 (was 80)
→ model_dim = 192
- feed_forward_dim = 384 (was 256)
- num_layers = 4 (was 3)
- dropout = 0.15 (was 0.10)
- weight_decay = 5e-4 (was 3e-4)
- batch_size = 16 (paper-faithful)
- Adam lr 1e-3, MS [25, 40] γ 0.1, 200 epochs, patience 30

Expected outcome
----------------
If capacity-bound: test 60-min 3.30–3.34 with single seed. If structurally
plateaued: ≈ 3.34 unchanged or slightly worse.

Kill criterion
--------------
Standard: val_mae no improvement for 15 epochs → early-stop.
Architecture-kill: if at epoch 20 the val 60-min is worse than 3.18, abandon
this size and try a different one (or skip ahead to R03 if running).

Wall-clock estimate
-------------------
~40–50 min on H200 (bigger model + 4 layers).
