R01 — Extended-seed STAEformer ensemble (seeds 4, 5, 6, 7) with hyperparam diversity
=====================================================================================

Hypothesis
----------
The existing 4-seed test-60min std is 0.004. Adding 4 more *identical-config*
seeds gives diminishing returns (~−0.005 to −0.01). To get a real ensemble win
we need *hyperparam diversity*, not just different RNG init. Vary dropout and
batch_size across seeds 4–7 to create modestly different optima.

Setup
-----
4 new runs, all paper-faithful otherwise (Adam lr=1e-3, wd=3e-4, MS [20,30] γ
0.1, max 200 epochs, patience 30, batch 16, masked MAE):

  seed 4: dropout=0.10 (baseline), batch_size=16 (baseline)  -> stae_R01_s4
  seed 5: dropout=0.05 (less reg)  , batch_size=16            -> stae_R01_s5
  seed 6: dropout=0.15 (more reg)  , batch_size=16            -> stae_R01_s6
  seed 7: dropout=0.10              , batch_size=32 (smoother)-> stae_R01_s7

Expected outcome
----------------
8-seed ensemble (4 existing + 4 new) → test 60-min ≈ 3.275–3.285 with ST-TTC.
If the diverse-hyperparam seeds give larger spread than the identical-config seeds,
the gain is in the −0.005 to −0.015 range on top of the current 3.283.

Kill criterion
--------------
Per-seed: val_mae no improvement for 15 epochs → early-stop the run.
Overall: if 4 new seeds all individually land worse than 3.36 60-min test (vs
3.34 for the baseline seeds), the diversity hurt → revert to identical-config.

Wall-clock estimate
-------------------
~30 min/seed × 4 sequential on H200 = ~2 hours.

Run command (background, on remote)
-----------------------------------
nohup bash scripts/run_R01_seeds.sh > logs/R01_master.log 2>&1 &
